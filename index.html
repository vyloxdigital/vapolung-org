8 VapoLung Ingredients, Fully Explained

Every compound in VapoLung is selected for its relevance to respiratory wellness and backed by published research. Dosages appear as provided per recommended serving. For clinical studies on these ingredients, see the reference links at the NIH National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute and NIH NCCIH.

01 Cordyceps CS-4 Extract 80 mg ▾

Cordyceps Sinensis CS-4 is an adaptogenic fungus with a long history in traditional Tibetan and Chinese medicine as a lung tonic. Modern studies suggest Cordyceps may support oxygen utilization efficiency, pulmonary capacity, and resistance to respiratory fatigue. Its beta-glucan content is believed to modulate immune activity in the bronchial epithelium.

Research →
02 Green Tea EGCG Extract 60 mg ▾

Epigallocatechin gallate (EGCG) is the primary bioactive catechin in green tea. It is a potent antioxidant studied for its ability to protect respiratory epithelial cells from oxidative stress. Research published in peer-reviewed journals suggests EGCG may help inhibit inflammatory mediators in pulmonary tissue, supporting airway clarity during seasonal challenges.

Research →
03 Thyme Leaf Extract (Thymol) 50 mg ▾

Thyme (Thymus vulgaris) has been used as a respiratory herb for centuries across European herbal traditions. Its active compound thymol functions as a natural expectorant and bronchial muscle relaxant. The NIH NCCIH maintains extensive documentation on thyme's traditional and contemporary uses in respiratory wellness.

Research →
04 Pine Bark Extract 40 mg ▾

Pine bark extract is rich in oligomeric proanthocyanidins (OPCs), powerful polyphenolic antioxidants. Multiple clinical studies have explored its anti-inflammatory effects in airway tissue. OPCs may help reduce airway inflammation and improve blood flow to lung tissue, contributing to overall respiratory comfort and pulmonary defense.

Research →
05 Andrographis Paniculata 35 mg ▾

Known as the "King of Bitters," Andrographis paniculata is a traditional South Asian medicinal herb. Its key constituent, andrographolide, has been studied for its immune-modulating and anti-inflammatory properties in the respiratory tract. Research indicates it may help support mucosal immunity in the upper airways during seasonal exposure periods.

Research →
06 Vitamin D3 (as Cholecalciferol) 25 mcg (1,000 IU) ▾

Vitamin D3 plays a critical role in maintaining the integrity of the respiratory epithelium and regulating immune responses within the lungs. The NIH Office of Dietary Supplements notes that adequate Vitamin D status is associated with healthy respiratory function, particularly in adult populations across all US climate zones.

NIH Fact Sheet →
07 Ginkgo Biloba Extract 30 mg ▾

Ginkgo biloba standardized extract (24% ginkgo flavone glycosides) is well-researched for its platelet-activating factor (PAF) inhibition and vasodilatory properties. In the respiratory context, these mechanisms may improve microcirculation in pulmonary tissue, supporting oxygen exchange efficiency and bronchial anti-inflammatory action. PubMed lists hundreds of peer-reviewed studies on Ginkgo's vascular and anti-inflammatory effects.

Research →
08 Zinc Bisglycinate 5 mg ▾

Zinc bisglycinate is a highly bioavailable chelated form of zinc. Zinc plays a foundational role in respiratory mucosal defense — supporting the structural integrity of airway epithelial cells and modulating immune signaling in the bronchi. The NIH ODS Zinc Fact Sheet documents its importance in maintaining healthy immune function and epithelial barriers.

NIH Fact Sheet →
